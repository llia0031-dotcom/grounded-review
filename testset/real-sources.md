# Real ads: where they come from

The two real-ad sets (split `real`) are not redistributed: the ad descriptions are quoted from
regulator rulings and case reports. This file gives the selection rules, the counts and the source pages.
Results: `eval/results/real-2026-11-14.summary.md` and `eval/results/real-zh-2026-11-14.summary.md`;
plan and expectations: `eval/PREREGISTRATION.md`, "Real ads" sections.

## English: 30 rulings of the UK Advertising Standards Authority

Source: the ASA's published rulings, https://www.asa.org.uk/codes-and-rulings/rulings.html
(filter by decision and date).

- **23 Upheld** (answer: reject). From the 235 Upheld rulings published from 1 January to
  11 November 2026, most recent first, at most four per group: medical claims, weight and body image,
  investment returns, comparisons with competitors, ratings and superlatives, alcohol. Skipped:
  same-day near-duplicates; rulings whose issues fall outside the nine categories (prices,
  environmental claims, less-healthy food placement, prescription-only medicines, gambling, game
  footage); pet products. The weight group had three eligible rulings.
- **7 Not upheld** (answer: approve). Every Not upheld ruling from 1 January 2025 to 11 November 2026
  whose issue falls in the nine categories (4 of 37), plus the 3 such rulings from 2024 (of 14).
- Each item is the ruling's "Ad description" section with advertiser, product, competitor and
  person names replaced by placeholders. Categories come from the issues the ASA upheld.
- 8 items are marked evidence-dependent: the ruling turned on evidence the ad does not show.
- Mapping of upheld issues to the nine categories: disease, treatment and medicinal claims (CAP
  12.x, 15.6.2) to MEDICAL; weight control and body image (13.x, and 1.3 where it concerns the body)
  to WEIGHT; before-and-after pictures to BEFORE_AFTER; investment returns and risk warnings (14.x) to
  GUARANTEE; comparisons with named competitors and denigration (3.32 to 3.35) to DISPARAGE;
  superlatives to ABSOLUTE; ratings, review counts and statistics to DATA; alcohol (18.x, BCAP 19.x)
  to ALCOHOL. Issues outside the nine categories get no label.

## Chinese: 16 published cases

- **12 penalised** (answer: reject): every case in the State Administration for Market Regulation's
  typical illegal-advertising releases of 31 January 2026 and 21 July 2026 that quotes the ad's words
  and whose words fall in the nine categories. Each item keeps the product type and the quoted words.
  Releases as reprinted with their source named:
  https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260131/b7a71709bc5f4ec2bf6dff05ce08486d/c.html and
  https://www.rmzxw.com.cn/c/2026-07-22/3951084.shtml
- **4 found lawful** (answer: approve): published cases from 2015 to 2019 in which a regulator or a
  court found that a superlative did not break Article 9(3) of the Advertising Law. Sources: the
  People's Court case database entry on an award name containing 最佳 (reprinted at
  https://www.055110.com/xz/6/537.html), and the cases cited in Shi Lidong, "绝对化广告用语的区分处罚"
  (http://iolaw.cssn.cn/fxyjdt/201906/t20190625_4924016.shtml).
- 3 items are marked evidence-dependent.
